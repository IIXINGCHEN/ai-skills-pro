## What it does

Actively builds and sharpens the project's domain model while designing: challenging terms, inventing edge-case scenarios, and writing the glossary and decisions down the moment they crystallise.

## When to reach for it

Type /domain-modeling, or the agent reaches for it when terminology is fuzzy, two people mean different things by one word, or an ADR needs recording.
