## Render-safe wording

Image models print any label they read. Name zones by position words only, and close every prompt with: "The only text anywhere on the poster is the Chinese text quoted above, rendered exactly, sharp and legible. Keep the canvas free of labels, English letters, extra numbers, watermarks, QR codes, and disclaimers."
