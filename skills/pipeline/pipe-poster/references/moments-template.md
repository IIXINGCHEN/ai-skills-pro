# Moments post template

Four beats, 80 to 160 characters, two to four emoji at line starts.

```text
<Hook: the pain in the customer's own words, one line>

<The offer in one line>:
✓ <KEY_POINT 1 as a full phrase>
✓ <KEY_POINT 2>
✓ <KEY_POINT 3>

<How pricing works, in provable words. The zero-risk promise.>

<One line of extra value: environment, time saved, peace of mind>

<CTA: what to send, and what happens next>👇
📍服务范围：[城市/区域]
📞微信/电话：[联系方式]
```
